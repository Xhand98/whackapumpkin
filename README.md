# Whack-A-Pumpkin!
Whack-A-Pumpkin (Whack is a typo, it was meant to be Whac) is a <a href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whac-A-Mole">Whac-A-Mole</a> type game with a Halloween aesthetic! You play as a mallet traveling through an empty,
field of yours, your goal is to smash the most Pumpkins you can before they consume YOU!

### To do so you're provided a set of controls that are:
Move mouse: Travel around the field.
Click: Smash a Mole
Click: Shoot "Halloween treats"

### It is important to know the rules of the game, and they are the following: 
- If 3 Pumpkins spawn and despawn without being removed (clicked) they consume you, aka you lost.
- Every pumpkin you Whack grants you with 1 point to your score.
- Every 10 pumpkins you Whack gets you one level more of difficulty (despawn speed of the pumpkins).

### The level system is designed around this table:

| Score    | Time alive |
| -------- | -------    |
| 00 - 10  | 45 frames  |
| 11 - 20  | 30 frames  |
| 21 - 30  | 15 frames  |
| 30 - ∞   | 10 frames  |
